ABOUT

I'm Daniil, a filmmaker and cinematographer based out of Bellevue, WA. I'm drawn to the metaphysical yet simple attributes of life and its stories.

My work spans short films, music videos, commercials, and documentaries. I care about honest frames, intentional pacing, and the kind of images that can give language to the unsaid.

My favorite movie is La La Land. *spoiler alert* Why couldn't they just end up together?
